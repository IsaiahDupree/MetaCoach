## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Sign Up**: Create your MetaCoach account with your email address
2. **Connect Meta Account**: Authorize access to your Meta Ads Manager account
   - Click "Connect Account" on the dashboard
   - Follow the OAuth flow to grant permissions
   - Select which ad accounts to link
3. **Verify Connection**: Your ad accounts will appear in the account selector

### First Login

On your first login, MetaCoach will:
- Sync your campaigns, ad sets, and ads
- Fetch historical performance data (last 30 days)
- Generate initial recommendations
- Set up default alert thresholds

## Performance Analytics

### Understanding the Metrics

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**
- Formula: Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
- Goal: >2.0x (earn $2+ per $1 spent)
- Industry averages: 2.0x - 4.0x

**CTR (Click-Through Rate)**
- Formula: Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100
- Good: >1.5%
- Average: 0.8% - 1.5%

**CPC (Cost Per Click)**
- Formula: Spend ÷ Clicks
- Lower is better
- Benchmark: $0.50 - $3.00

**CPA (Cost Per Action)**
- Formula: Spend ÷ Conversions
- Depends on industry and product
- Monitor against your conversion value

### Date Range Selection

- **7 Days**: Short-term trends, rapid optimization
- **30 Days**: Monthly performance, standard reporting
- **90 Days**: Quarterly trends, seasonal patterns

### Viewing Campaign Details

1. Click any campaign name in the table
2. See campaign-specific metrics and trends
3. View associated ad sets and ads
4. Access campaign-level recommendations

## Coaching Recommendations

### How Recommendations Work

MetaCoach analyzes your performance data and generates recommendations based on:
- Historical performance trends
- Industry benchmarks
- Campaign structure and setup
- Audience and creative performance

### Recommendation Types

**Budget Optimization**
- Increase budget for high-performing campaigns
- Reallocate from underperformers
- Optimize daily spend pacing

**Creative Refresh**
- Replace underperforming creatives
- Test new formats and messaging
- Combat audience fatigue

**Audience Expansion**
- Create lookalike audiences
- Expand winning audience segments
- Test new interest/demographic combinations

**Targeting & Setup**
- Adjust bid strategies
- Refine audience targeting
- Improve campaign structure

### Implementing Recommendations

1. Click a recommendation to view details
2. Read the explanation and data
3. Review implementation steps
4. Click "Implement" or "Open in Meta Ads Manager"
5. Follow the step-by-step instructions
6. Return to mark recommendation as completed

### Recommendation Confidence Score

- **90-100%**: High confidence, safe to implement
- **75-89%**: Medium-high confidence, monitor results
- **50-74%**: Lower confidence, test first
- <50%: Informational only, requires validation

## Reports & Automation

### Generating Reports

1. Go to **Reports** section
2. Click **Generate Report**
3. Select date range
4. Choose report type:
   - Executive Summary (quick overview)
   - Detailed Analysis (full metrics and charts)
   - Client Report (branded for sharing)
5. Click **Generate**

### Report Contents

- Executive summary of key achievements
- Performance metrics summary
- Campaign analysis and rankings
- Trend charts and visualizations
- Top recommendations

### Exporting Reports

**PDF Download**
1. Open generated report
2. Click **Export as PDF**
3. File downloads to your computer

**Email Delivery**
1. Click **Send via Email**
2. Enter recipient email addresses
3. Choose to attach PDF
4. Click **Send**

### Report Scheduling

1. Go to **Settings** > **Report Schedule**
2. Click **Create Schedule**
3. Select frequency (Daily/Weekly/Monthly)
4. Choose send time and day
5. Add recipient email addresses
6. Click **Enable**

**Schedule Management**
- View all scheduled reports
- Edit frequency and recipients
- Pause/resume schedules
- Delete schedules

## Alerts & Monitoring

### Understanding Alerts

Alerts notify you of significant changes or issues:

**Critical Alerts**
- Budget overspend (red)
- Severe performance drops (>30%)
- Failed account syncs

**Warning Alerts**
- Performance decline (20-30%)
- Approaching budget limits (80%+)
- Unusual patterns detected

**Info Alerts**
- Significant performance improvements
- New optimization opportunities

### Alert Settings

1. Go to **Settings** > **Alerts**
2. Configure:
   - Email recipients
   - Alert sensitivity
   - Budget alert threshold
   - Performance thresholds

### Acknowledging Alerts

1. Click notification or visit **Alerts** page
2. Review alert details
3. Click **Acknowledge** to mark as read
4. Recommended action appears below alert

### Email Alerts

- Sent immediately for critical alerts
- Sent daily digest for warnings
- Can be customized per alert type

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: How often is my data updated?**
A: Dashboard data refreshes every 15-30 minutes. Reports can be generated on-demand, and scheduled reports run at specified times.

**Q: Can I connect multiple Meta ad accounts?**
A: Yes, click "Add Account" and authorize each account. Switch between them using the dropdown.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication and never store your login credentials.

### Performance & Recommendations

**Q: Why is my ROAS shown as very high?**
A: ROAS is calculated as revenue/spend. With low spend but high conversions, it can appear inflated. Wait for more data for stability.

**Q: When should I implement a recommendation?**
A: Implement high-confidence (90%+) recommendations immediately. Test medium-confidence (75-89%) recommendations on a subset of budget first.

**Q: Can I ignore a recommendation?**
A: Yes, click "Dismiss" on any recommendation. It won't appear again unless conditions change.

### Reports & Alerts

**Q: Can I customize my scheduled reports?**
A: Yes, in Settings > Report Schedule, you can modify format, recipients, frequency, and content.

**Q: How do I disable alerts?**
A: Go to Settings > Alerts and toggle individual alert types off, or disable all alerts.

**Q: What time zone are reports sent?**
A: Reports use your account's time zone, set in Settings > Account Settings.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: Data isn't updating**
A: Try refreshing the page or disconnecting/reconnecting your account.

**Q: Can't see my campaigns**
A: Ensure you've:
1. Authorized all necessary permissions
2. Selected the correct account in dropdown
3. Account has active campaigns

**Q: Recommendations seem inaccurate**
A: Recommendations improve with more data. They need at least 7 days of performance history to be reliable.
